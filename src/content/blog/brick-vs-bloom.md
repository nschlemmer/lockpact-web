Brick and Bloom do the same job: a physical object you tap with your phone to start and end a lock on your distracting apps. Bloom is cheaper, Brick is simpler, and both leave you stuck when you forget the thing at home. If you want the same idea without hardware, [LockPact](/go/b-brick-vs-bloom/) is free to use and puts a partner where the device would be.

## What is the difference between Brick and Bloom?

Brick is a $59 device; Bloom is a $39 keycard or a $59 magnetic pod. Both work by tapping your phone to start a lock and again to end it, both list no subscription and a 30-day money-back guarantee, and both give you emergency exits that unlock your apps without the device. The differences are price, shape and how many emergency exits you get.

| | Brick | Bloom |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $59 (Case $39, Mount $15) | Card $39, Pod $59 |
| Shape | A device you tap your phone against | A stainless steel keycard, or a magnetic pod you can stick on a surface |
| Subscription | None listed | None listed ("lifetime access" with purchase) |
| Emergency exits | Five Emergency Unbricks | Three emergency exits, plus one more each month |
| Phones | iPhone (iOS 18 or later) and Android 12 or later | iPhone and Android |
| Money-back guarantee | 30 days | 30 days |
| Free shipping | On orders of two or more | On orders over $75 |

Prices and policies are from each company's own store on October 16, 2026, and can change.

## Which one is better for you?

Pick Brick if you want the plainest version of the idea and don't mind the higher price. Pick Bloom if you want to spend less, want a card that fits in a wallet, or want a pod you can leave on a desk, nightstand or fridge. Choose by where you'll keep the thing, because the lock only works when you can reach it on purpose and can't when you're tempted.

### Who should pick Brick?

People who want one device with one job. Brick's listing is short on extras: you pick the apps in its app, tap your phone against it to start, and rescan it to end. It also sells a Case and a Mount if you want somewhere to keep it.

### Who should pick Bloom?

People who want the cheaper option or a form factor that travels. The Bloom Card is $39 and fits in a wallet, and the Pod ($59) sticks to a surface so one spot in your home becomes the place you unlock. Its listing also mentions focus schedules and break timers.

## What happens when you forget or lose it?

You use an emergency exit, and then you run out. Brick lists five Emergency Unbricks that unblock all apps. Bloom lists three emergency exits and one more each month. After that, if the device is gone, the lock has nothing to end it with. That's the one situation hardware locks handle worst: forgetting it at home, losing it, or traveling without it.
